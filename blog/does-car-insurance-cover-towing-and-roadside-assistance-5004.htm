Does Car Insurance Cover Towing and Roadside Assistance?

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Blog Details

When you’re stranded on the side of the road with a flat tire or a dead battery, the last thing you want to worry about is whether your car insurance will cover the cost of towing or roadside assistance. In today’s fast-paced world, where unexpected breakdowns can disrupt your schedule and budget, understanding your auto insurance policy’s coverage for these services is crucial.

The Basics of Towing and Roadside Assistance Coverage

Not all car insurance policies automatically include towing and roadside assistance. These services are often offered as optional add-ons or included in more comprehensive coverage plans. Here’s what you need to know:

What Does Roadside Assistance Typically Cover?

Roadside assistance programs vary by provider, but most include:
- Towing to the nearest repair facility (usually up to a certain mileage limit).
- Battery jump-starts if your car won’t start.
- Flat tire changes (if you have a spare).
- Lockout services if you’ve locked your keys inside the car.
- Fuel delivery if you run out of gas.

Does Basic Liability Insurance Cover Towing?

No. Basic liability insurance only covers damages you cause to others in an accident—not your own vehicle. If you want coverage for towing or roadside emergencies, you’ll need to add it to your policy or purchase a separate plan.

Why Roadside Assistance Matters in Today’s World

With the rise of electric vehicles (EVs), increased urban congestion, and extreme weather events due to climate change, roadside assistance has become more relevant than ever.

The EV Factor

EV owners may assume they don’t need traditional roadside assistance since electric cars have fewer moving parts. However, EVs can still experience:
- Battery depletion (especially in cold weather).
- Charging station malfunctions.
- Software glitches that immobilize the vehicle.

Many EV manufacturers include roadside assistance, but it’s wise to check whether your auto insurance offers specialized support for electric cars.

Climate Change and Extreme Weather

Hurricanes, floods, and wildfires are becoming more frequent, leading to an increase in vehicle breakdowns. Roadside assistance can be a lifesaver if:
- Your car stalls in a flood.
- Extreme heat causes engine failure.
- Snow and ice leave you stranded.

Some insurers now offer expanded emergency services for weather-related incidents, so review your policy before disaster strikes.

Comparing Insurance Providers: Who Offers the Best Roadside Assistance?

Not all roadside assistance programs are created equal. Here’s how major insurers stack up:

Progressive

  • Offers emergency roadside service as an add-on.
  • Covers towing, jump-starts, and lockouts.
  • No mileage limit for towing if the breakdown occurs within 100 miles of home.

State Farm

  • Provides 24/7 roadside assistance with a per-service fee.
  • Includes fuel delivery and winching (if stuck off-road).
  • Available for both personal and commercial policies.

AAA (Not an Insurer but a Popular Alternative)

  • Membership-based with tiered plans.
  • Offers longer towing distances (up to 200 miles with premium plans).
  • Additional perks like trip interruption coverage and discounts.

Hidden Costs and Limitations

Before relying on your insurance for roadside help, be aware of potential drawbacks:

Service Caps and Fees

  • Some policies limit the number of service calls per year (e.g., 3-4).
  • You may still pay a deductible or service fee per incident.

Exclusions

  • Pre-existing mechanical issues may not be covered.
  • If you’re off-roading or using your car for rideshare, your claim could be denied.

Alternatives to Insurance-Based Roadside Assistance

If your policy doesn’t include towing or the coverage is limited, consider:
- Manufacturer programs (many new cars come with complimentary roadside assistance).
- Credit card perks (some premium cards offer free towing).
- Third-party services like AAA or Better World Club.

Final Thoughts

In an era where mobility is essential, ensuring you have reliable towing and roadside assistance can save you time, money, and stress. Review your car insurance policy today—don’t wait until you’re stuck on the highway with no help in sight.
