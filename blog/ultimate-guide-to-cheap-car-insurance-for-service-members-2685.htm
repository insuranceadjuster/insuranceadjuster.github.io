Ultimate Guide to Cheap Car Insurance for Service Members

  • Home
  • Blog
  • Blog Details

Finding affordable car insurance as a service member can be a challenge, especially with frequent relocations, deployments, and unique driving patterns. However, with the right strategies, you can secure cheap car insurance without sacrificing coverage. This guide covers everything you need to know, from discounts tailored for military personnel to navigating policies during overseas deployments.

Why Service Members Need Specialized Car Insurance

Military life comes with unique circumstances that affect car insurance needs. Frequent moves, long periods of inactivity (during deployments), and high-risk postings can complicate traditional insurance policies. Here’s why service members should seek specialized coverage:

Frequent Relocations Impact Rates

Insurance costs vary by state and even by ZIP code. Moving from a low-risk area to a high-crime or high-accident zone can spike premiums. Some insurers offer "military relocation discounts" to offset these changes.

Deployments and Storage Discounts

If you’re deployed and your car is in storage, you may qualify for a "storage discount" or reduced coverage. Some insurers allow you to suspend certain coverages (like liability) while keeping comprehensive protection.

High-Risk Assignments

Service members stationed in areas with extreme weather, high theft rates, or poor road conditions may face higher premiums. Specialized military insurers understand these risks and offer better terms.

Top Discounts for Service Members

Insurance companies offer exclusive discounts for active-duty personnel, veterans, and even family members. Here are the best ones to look for:

1. Military/Defense Discount

Many insurers (like USAA, GEICO, and Progressive) offer discounts ranging from 5% to 15% for active-duty, reservists, and veterans.

2. Low-Mileage Discount

If you’re deployed or live on base, you may drive fewer miles than civilians. Providers like State Farm offer discounts for low annual mileage.

3. Good Student Discount

If you or a dependent on your policy maintains a B average or higher, you could save up to 25%.

4. Multi-Policy Discount

Bundling auto insurance with renters or homeowners insurance can cut costs by 10% to 20%.

5. Safe Driver Programs

Usage-based insurance (UBI) programs track driving habits and reward safe behavior with discounts.

Best Insurance Providers for Service Members

Not all insurers cater to military needs. Here are the top picks:

USAA – Best for Active-Duty and Veterans

Exclusive to military families, USAA offers unbeatable rates, deployment-friendly policies, and stellar customer service.

GEICO – Best for Affordability

GEICO’s military discount (up to 15%) and flexible payment plans make it a top choice for budget-conscious service members.

Armed Forces Insurance (AFI) – Best for Overseas Coverage

AFI specializes in policies for overseas deployments and high-risk assignments.

Progressive – Best for High-Risk Drivers

If you have a less-than-perfect record, Progressive’s Snapshot program can help lower rates over time.

How to Lower Your Premiums Further

Beyond discounts, these strategies can help you save:

Increase Your Deductible

Raising your deductible from $500 to $1,000 can reduce premiums by 10% to 25%.

Improve Your Credit Score

In most states, insurers use credit-based insurance scores. Paying bills on time and reducing debt can lower rates.

Take a Defensive Driving Course

Some insurers offer discounts for completing an approved course.

Compare Quotes Annually

Rates change frequently. Shopping around before renewal can uncover better deals.

Handling Insurance During Deployment

Deployments complicate insurance, but these steps ensure you’re covered without overpaying:

1. Notify Your Insurer

Some providers allow you to suspend liability coverage while keeping comprehensive protection.

2. Consider Storage Insurance

If your car won’t be driven for months, storage insurance is cheaper than full coverage.

3. Power of Attorney (POA)

If someone else manages your car, ensure they’re listed on the policy to avoid coverage gaps.

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

Letting Policies Lapse

A coverage gap can lead to higher rates later. Even during deployment, maintain at least comprehensive coverage.

Ignoring State Requirements

Each state has minimum coverage laws. Ensure your policy complies, especially after a PCS move.

Overlooking USAA Eligibility

Many service members assume USAA is only for active-duty, but veterans and family members may also qualify.

Final Thoughts

Securing cheap car insurance as a service member requires research and leveraging military-specific benefits. By exploring discounts, choosing the right provider, and adjusting coverage during deployments, you can save hundreds annually. Stay proactive, compare options, and never settle for overpriced policies.
